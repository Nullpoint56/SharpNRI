## Optional inputs, history, motion and sharpening

QUALITY mode, 512x288 output. **HDR**: relative deviation of an HDR constant (8, 4, 2) with an
exposure texture and a pre-exposure of 2. **Reactive**: deviation of the constant with an all-zero
reactive mask in R8_UNORM / R16_SFLOAT. **Reset**: deviation from a new colour one frame after
the change, with RESET_HISTORY / without (only the first is asserted). **Edge**: a vertical edge
moving 2 render pixels a frame with matching motion vectors, worst row's distance from where the
scene puts it in output pixels (asserted at most 2), and the flat sides' deviation. **Sharpness**:
output amplitude of stripes of input amplitude 0.1 at sharpness 0 / 1 (the types with a setting).
